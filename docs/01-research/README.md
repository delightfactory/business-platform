# Research Register

This directory records research that materially informs product or architecture decisions.

## Research streams

### Product and market

- HR/payroll product patterns;
- MENA/Egypt HR and payroll expectations;
- modular SaaS packaging and employee self-service patterns;
- attendance, geofence, and biometric integration patterns.

### Architecture

- multi-tenant isolation models;
- entitlement versus authorization design;
- shared versus dedicated tenant infrastructure;
- modular monolith and future domain expansion patterns.

### Compliance

- employment/payroll statutory requirements for supported jurisdictions;
- personal-data and sensitive-data requirements;
- versioning requirements for statutory rules and finalized payroll outcomes.

### Internal reuse

- reusable platform concepts and implementation patterns from prior internal SaaS systems;
- explicit rejection of domain-specific or technically indebted legacy components where reuse would be unsafe.

## Research-to-decision rule

Research is evidence, not automatically a product requirement. A market pattern or competitor feature becomes a platform decision only when its relevance to target customers and the active product strategy is established and the decision is recorded.

## Public repository rule

This repository may be public during early development. Research notes must therefore avoid confidential customer data, private commercial terms, credentials, or copying proprietary implementation details from private donor systems. Internal reuse audits may describe patterns and classifications without publishing sensitive source material.
