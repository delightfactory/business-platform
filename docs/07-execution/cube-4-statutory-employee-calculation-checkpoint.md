# Cube 4 composed employee statutory calculation

The private `payroll.calculate_statutory_employee` composes an operational employee result, explicit reviewed current/prior tax facts and current insurance obligations using the same dated stored pack. It validates complete operational amounts against their earning/deduction/employer-cost lines before calculation. The current pack-designated deductible employee insurance portion reduces CURRENT taxable earnings; prior net remains net of its prior deductions. Nontaxable earnings stay in gross. Operational deductions do not automatically reduce tax income.

The result preserves original lines/issues, appends distinct employee-insurance/employer-insurance/tax explanations, and separates operational deductions, statutory deductions, employer contributions, extra employer costs and total employer expense. Prior assessed-tax refunds remain signed. A negative resulting balance has an owned blocker and `net:null`, while `calculated_net` explains the negative amount. Reapplication is rejected to prevent duplicate statutory lines.

An insured employee requires the source-month insurance context. An explicit not-insured status requires a reviewed exclusion reference and absent insurance payload; absent status/wage is not an exemption and cannot imply zero. Unknown prior net, excess precision, unsupported tax treatment/duration and unqualified packs are rejected. The function is private SECURITY INVOKER, empty search_path, with API-role EXECUTE revoked.

This is the composed numeric worker, not public candidate integration or statutory qualification. It deliberately keeps `financially_qualified:false`. Authoritative source attribution, current taxable treatment, obligation ownership/consumption and governed legal pack issuance must be implemented before a candidate can use these results to become financially ready. No ordinary UI can submit arbitrary taxable/net facts to this function. Current official2026 comparisons remain unavailable per the owner and are not replaced by these tests.
