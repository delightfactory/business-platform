# Cube 4 cumulative tax adapter checkpoint

The dated private `payroll.calculate_cumulative_tax` adapter calculates a complete cumulative tax delta from a stored pack and explicit cumulative employee facts. It selects the pack-defined income column, annualizes the reviewed cumulative net before personal exemption, applies the annual personal exemption once, floors the annual base to ten, evaluates validated marginal bands, scales back by source duration, rounds cumulative tax to cents, and subtracts prior assessed tax. A negative delta remains a correction/refund rather than silently becoming zero. Prior withheld cash is not accepted as assessed tax.

Rules are stored on the immutable pack. No law constants, verified pack, public command or candidate net were seeded/exposed. The adapter is revoked from PUBLIC, anon, authenticated and service_role. The only supported algorithm is `eg-cumulative-tax-v1`, not an arbitrary formula engine. Explicit pack choices govern raw versus floored column selection and cumulative rounding. Supporting a choice does not establish its legal applicability.

Input earning dates must share one tax year and fall wholly within the pack's effective interval. Cross-year and changing-pack attribution are rejected pending the integrated earning allocation contract. Source duration is never derived from payment date or salary-cycle length; zero/unknown duration and unsupported tax treatments are rejected. Insurance deductions and earning attribution must be qualified upstream before these explicit inputs may be generated from a candidate.

Primary source context: [ETA FAQ v4 March2026, question15](https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-05/faqs-payroll-system-v4.pdf) describes cumulative annualization, rescaling and subtraction of prior due tax; [Law7/2024](https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.7-2024.pdf) provides dated Article8 schedules. Both were reopened during this implementation. The adapter's test schedules and expected values are independently derived NONLEGAL fixtures, not official2026 model results. A populated review-evidence field alone cannot establish external legal qualification; governed pack issuance still requires the full Frozen official comparison and applicability gates.
