## Limits and open gates

- **States not seen:**

  - error and toast states
  - the activate/deactivate pending state
  - where focus goes after the pending guard reopens a form
  - org empty, failed and retry
  - the org 768 capture (only Codex inspected it)
- **Not visually confirmed:** the native pending guard and input retention passed their earlier test, but I haven't seen them on screen. Behaviour with JavaScript off hasn't been observed.
- **Runtime:** real roles, provider, mutations, financial and privacy checks, and the history of existing import attempts are still open.
- **Scroll measurement:** the catalog heading moved from y 2243.42 to 399.20. That shows less scrolling only, not task completion or speed.
- **Plan order:** core R0–R8 comes first. D16 is deferred and D2/D3/D8 are pending.
